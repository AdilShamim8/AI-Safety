# Take-home: the dossier — the P10 format

**Navigate:** [← take-homes](README.md) · [safety engineer](../roles/safety-engineer.md)

**The prompt as issued.** "You are the safety engineer for this
deployment. Produce the go/no-go document." The senior take-home:
the whole pipeline in one artifact.

**The mapping.** The capstone
[P10](../../projects/p10-capstone-agent-evaluation-and-assurance-dossier/README.md)
— evaluation, monitoring, decision, and the honest tiers in one
document; the P01→P10 arc is the dossier's training wheels.

**The rubric.**
1. **Structure survives scrutiny.** Scope → evidence → decision →
   monitoring → tiers, in that order; a dossier that hides the
   decision until page 9 reads as evasion.
2. **Every number carries its provenance.** Case counts, intervals,
   access levels — the registers' discipline applied to a deployment
   document.
3. **Go/no-go criteria fixed in advance.** The preregistration habit
   at deployment scale: thresholds written before the results, or
   explicitly labeled post-hoc.
4. **The rollback path.** What is watched, what triggers, who acts —
   the [P08](../../projects/p08-control-and-monitoring-protocol/README.md)
   pattern integrated, not bolted on.

**The honest tiers expected.** The dossier's final section IS the
tiers: what this decision rests on, what was not verified, what
would have to change it. A dossier without that section is a
brochure.
