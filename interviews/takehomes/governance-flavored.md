# Take-home: the governance format — the ISO/EU flavor

**Navigate:** [← take-homes](README.md) · [auditor role](../roles/auditor.md)

**The prompt as issued.** "Review this deployment's conformance
claims against ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act. Produce the gap
report." Rare outside compliance-heavy teams, but when it appears it
decides the offer: this format cannot be improvised.

**The mapping.** The standards spine already registered: ISO/IEC
42001:2023 (SRC-REPORT-050, metadata-only — the management-system
standard, organizational not model-level), the EU AI Act timeline
(SRC-REPORT-012: in force 2024-08-01, GPAI obligations since
2025-08-02, general applicability 2026-08-02), NIST AI 600-1, and
the lab frameworks (SRC-REPORT-048/049). Route:
[audit-only](../../roadmaps/audit-only.md).

**The rubric.**
1. **Instruments separated by layer.** National guidance (NIST),
   certifiable management systems (ISO), binding law (EU), voluntary
   lab commitments — conflating layers is the classic fail.
2. **Dates quoted with the claim.** "GPAI obligations since
   2025-08-02" — a governance take-home without dates is a
   paraphrase.
3. **Gap report ranked by risk.** Not a checklist dump: which gaps
   are blocking, which are cosmetic, which need evidence the
   organization does not have.
4. **The conformance-vs-evidence distinction.** A claim of
   conformance is not evidence of conformance — the register's
   metadata-only discipline is the same lesson.

**The honest tiers expected.** Standards not accessible in full text
stated as such (ISO is paywalled — the register says metadata-only);
the review's conclusions carrying that limit openly.
