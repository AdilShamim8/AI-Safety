# The evaluator role — the adjacent family

**Navigate:** [← roles](README.md) · [route](../../roadmaps/evaluations-and-assurance.md)

**The day-to-day question.** *What can this model actually do, and
what does it fail at?* — answered in capability reports, subgroup
analyses, and the refusal to average away the tails.

## Why "adjacent"

The careers index observes three core families hiring under their own
titles; the evaluator family is the shaped edge between the researcher
and the engineer — every lab's safety team and every eval org
(observed employer shapes in the careers index) staffs it, and the
[evaluations-and-assurance](../../roadmaps/evaluations-and-assurance.md)
route trains it without renaming itself. The capstone fit: **P09
(shift-subgroup reliability)** — the project whose entire point is
that aggregate metrics hide subgroup failures.

- [The demand signal](../market/demand-signal.md) and [skills

## The interview-loop emphasis

Expect the project deep-dive bank to press on **P09 specifically**:
what shifted, which subgroups, what the confidence intervals were,
and what you would do with more compute. The [take-homes](../takehomes/README.md)
layer maps the audit format onto this family.
