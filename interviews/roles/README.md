# The roles — five families, one spine

**Navigate:** [← interviews](../README.md) · [careers index](../../roadmaps/careers-index.md)

**What this sub-layer is.** The role families this repository trains,
each page answering four things: the day-to-day question, the route
and capstones that train it, the market pages that price its demand,
and its interview-loop emphasis.

| Family | Day-to-day question | Route | Capstone |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Safety researcher](safety-researcher.md) | why does it fail — and what does that teach us? | [alignment research](../../roadmaps/alignment-research.md) | P06 + Ch.24 proposal |
| [Security specialist](security-specialist.md) | how does the adversary get in through the model layer? | [AI security engineering](../../roadmaps/ai-security-engineering.md) | P07 + P10 |
| [Safety engineer](safety-engineer.md) | safe enough to ship — and how would we know? | [evaluations and assurance](../../roadmaps/evaluations-and-assurance.md) | P01→P10 arc |
| [Evaluator](evaluator.md) | what can it do, and what does it fail at? | [evaluations and assurance](../../roadmaps/evaluations-and-assurance.md) | P09 |
| [Auditor](auditor.md) | does it meet the standard it claims? | [audit-only](../../roadmaps/audit-only.md) | the standards spine |

**Continuity note.** The careers index observes three core families
hiring under their own titles; the evaluator and auditor are the
adjacent families the routes also train — labeled as adjacent, never
as observed hiring shapes. All five share the methodological spine:
paired ledgers, Wilson intervals, preregistration, honest residuals —
all five jobs die the same death without it.
