This family's loop weights the research artifact heaviest: expect the
project deep-dive bank and the theory bank in
[questions](../questions/README.md), and take-home formats that read
like small studies. The entry mechanism is fellowships first (MATS:

report working in alignment — self-reported program figures, priced
as such in the careers index), then research artifacts over
credentials: preregistered questions and falsifiable results —
exactly what this repository's projects produce.
