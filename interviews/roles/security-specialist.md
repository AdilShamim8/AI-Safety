## The market that prices it

The strongest signal in the whole market layer: the [cyber workforce
gap](../market/workforce-gap.md) — 5.5M active, 4.8M gap, +19% YoY
(ISC2 2024). In the US, the [employment outlook](../market/employment-outlook.md):
median 129,180 dollars (May 2025), +21% projected 2025-2035 (BLS OOH).
The careers index adds the posting-level view: a SANS analysis of
10,643 postings maps ten AI-security roles, four hiring under their
own titles today (vendor-published, commercially motivated — the
caveat is recorded in the claim).
