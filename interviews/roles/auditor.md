# The auditor role — the governance family

**Navigate:** [← roles](README.md) · [route](../../roadmaps/audit-only.md) ·
[market: the workforce gap](../market/workforce-gap.md)

**The day-to-day question.** *Does this deployment meet the standard
it claims to meet?* — answered in audit evidence, conformance
statements, and gap reports that survive a regulator reading them.

## The route that trains it

[Audit-only](../../roadmaps/audit-only.md) — the route that reads the
corpus without running the heavy projects. The standards spine is
already registered in this repository: ISO/IEC 42001:2023
(SRC-REPORT-050, metadata-only), NIST AI 600-1 (existing register
entries), the EU AI Act timeline (SRC-REPORT-012), and the lab
governance frameworks — DeepMind's FSF 3.0/3.1 (SRC-REPORT-048) and
OpenAI's Preparedness Framework v2 (SRC-REPORT-049), both registered
this session; the [selections layer](../../selections/README.md) groups them.

## The market that prices it

- [The workforce gap](../market/workforce-gap.md) — governance
  staffing rides the same 4.8M-gap curve as the rest of the security
  function (ISC2 2024).
- [The skills outlook](../market/skills-outlook.md) — the 39% figure
  is the auditor's own product in reverse: organizations re-skilling
  into governance need exactly the evidence-discipline this course
  trains.

## The interview-loop emphasis

The governance bank in [questions](../questions/README.md) and the
EU-AI-Act/ISO-flavored take-homes in [take-homes](../takehomes/README.md).

to say "this conformance statement is not backed by evidence" — the
honesty round, made a job.
