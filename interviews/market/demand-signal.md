## The three figures

The Future of Jobs Report 2025 (published 2025-01-07, surveying
employers on the 2025-2030 horizon) states that by 2030:

- **170 million** new roles are projected to be created;
- **92 million** existing jobs are projected to be displaced;
- net job growth of **78 million** — and job disruption equating to
**22% of jobs** by 2030.

## What the numbers do and do not say

They are employer *expectations*, not outcomes: a survey of what
employers believe will happen to their workforce shape. Read them as
the demand signal's direction and size — the market's own statement
of where it intends to spend headcount — never as a guarantee for any
individual's outcome.

For this corpus the operative reading is narrower: the three role
families it trains (safety researcher, security specialist, safety
engineer) sit inside the created-and-displaced net both ways — AI
capability creates the roles, and AI capability is also exactly what
the safety and security functions exist to govern. The signal says the
*function* grows; it does not say any particular posting waits for any
particular candidate.

## How to use this page

1. Treat 170M/92M/78M as the size of the arena, not a target.
2. Pair it with the [skills outlook](skills-outlook.md): the
39% core-skills-change figure is the *reason* preparation beats
credential-collecting.
3. Then the [cyber workforce gap](workforce-gap.md): the security
family's demand is measured in millions of unfilled positions.
4. Keep the register entry beside any figure you quote: a number
without its source and date is exactly what this repository refuses
to ship.
