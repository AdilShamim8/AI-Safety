## The figures (Q3 2025 frame, self-reported data)

- AI-focused software engineers, US average: about **245,000** dollars
  a year;
- the gap inside one company: AI staff engineers at Intuit close to
  **917,000** vs about **515,000** for non-AI staff engineers;
- median trend for AI engineers: peak about 295,000 (March 2024),
  about 228,500 (January 2025), rebounding to about 277,000, settling
  around **260,000-269,000**;
- senior snapshots: about 635,000 at Snap, about 513,000 at Cruise;
  entry level at LinkedIn about 288,050 vs 225,000 non-AI.

## How to use this page

Compensation data is the most snapshot-sensitive layer of the market:
these are levels.fyi's tabulations of self-reported user data in a
Q3 2025 frame, not a survey with published methodology margins. Use
them for one thing honestly — the market pays a visible premium for
the AI-focused family, and the premium is worth preparing for — and
re-check the primary source before negotiating anything.
