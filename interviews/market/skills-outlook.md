# The skills outlook — 39% by 2030

**Source:** WEF, *Future of Jobs Report 2025 — Skills Outlook*

after a refused fetch; figure verified via captures of the page).

## The figure

Employers expect **39% of workers' core skills to change by 2030**
(Figure 3.1 on the chapter page) — down from 44% in the 2023 edition.
Two-fifths of the existing skill set, transformed or outdated, on a
five-year horizon.

## What it means for preparation

If the half-life of the specific skill is the variable, the durable
assets are the ones this repository trains deliberately:

- **The method, not the tool.** Reading a paper and turning it into a
  runnable, measured artifact survives every tool rotation; the
  24-chapter course is built as that method, repeated.
- **The evidence discipline.** Knowing what a number is worth (access
  level, date, register entry) is exactly the 39%-proof skill: it
  transfers across every role family in [roles](../roles/README.md).
- **The honest tiering.** Saying what was not run, with the reason, is
  the interview behavior that maps to the figure: employers adjusting
  to skill change need people who can state the boundary of what they
  know.
