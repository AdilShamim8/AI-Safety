## The figures

- **Information security analysts:** median annual wage
**129,180 US dollars** (May 2025); employment projected to grow
**21 percent from 2025 to 2035** — much faster than the average for
all occupations.
- **The C&IT group:** median annual wage **109,470** (May 2025),
against the all-occupations median of **50,980**.

## How to read a government projection

A projection is a conditional forecast: if the 2025 assumptions hold,
the 2035 employment level looks like this. It is the right anchor for
direction and relative size (the family grows; it pays well above the
national median), and the wrong instrument for individual planning on
its own — pair it with the [workforce gap](workforce-gap.md) (global
demand) and the [compensation notes](compensation-notes.md) (what the
market actually clears at, self-reported).
