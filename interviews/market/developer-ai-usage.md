## The figures

From the 2025 survey's AI section (page fetched in full, 2026-11-06):

- **84%** of respondents use or plan to use AI tools in their
  development process (up from 76% the year before);
- **50.6%** of professional developers use AI tools daily — stated as
  51% in the page narrative (17.4% weekly, 12.8% monthly or less);
- favorable sentiment about **60%** this year — down from 70%+ in
  2023 and 2024.

## Why this page is in the market layer

The 84% is the adoption curve the [skills outlook](skills-outlook.md)
describes, measured in tools. For interviews it cuts two ways:

1. **Fluency is assumed.** The question banks in
   [questions](../questions/README.md) treat AI-tool usage as a
   baseline working practice, and the corpus's own method — run the
   thing, measure it, state what was not run — is the durable half of
   that fluency.
2. **Skepticism is also the signal.** Sentiment cooling ten points
   while usage climbs is the market pricing in tools that
   under-deliver; the prepared candidate can say precisely what the
   tools are worth, which is exactly what the honesty-round bank
   trains.
