# The governance bank — the standards spine, named

**Navigate:** [← questions](README.md) · [auditor role](../roles/auditor.md)

**How this bank works.** Governance rounds test whether you can name
the instrument, its scope, and its date — not recite marketing
language. Every framework below is a registered source or claim.

## The questions

**Q1. "What are the main AI governance instruments, and what does each actually cover?"**
- **NIST AI RMF + AI 600-1** (registered): the Generative AI Profile —
  risk management functions applied to generative AI.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** (SRC-REPORT-050, metadata-only): the AI
  management-system standard — organizational, not model-level.
- **EU AI Act** (SRC-REPORT-012 timeline): in force 2024-08-01; GPAI
  obligations since 2025-08-02; general applicability 2026-08-02.
- **Lab frameworks**: DeepMind FSF 3.0/3.1 (SRC-REPORT-048; CCLs and,
  from 3.1, TCLs), OpenAI Preparedness v2 (SRC-REPORT-049; three
  tracked categories, High/Critical thresholds).
The strong answer separates the four layers: national guidance
(NIST), certifiable management systems (ISO), binding law (EU), and
voluntary lab commitments — and says which layer a given question
belongs to.

**Q2. "What changes for a company on 2026-08-02?"**
The EU AI Act's general applicability date (with the exceptions the
timeline claims record). The probe is always "and what does that mean
for a high-risk system specifically?" — obligations, not dates, are
the real answer.

**Q3. "CCL vs TCL — which document are you quoting?"**
FSF 3.1's addition (April 2026 update): Critical Capability Levels vs
the new Tracked Capability Levels. Quoting the construct correctly
with its version date is the pass signal; this bank insists on it
because quoting a framework is how governance rounds expose
borrowing.

**Q4. "What does ISO 42001 not do?"**
It does not evaluate models; it certifies the management system
around them. The register entry's metadata-only label is itself the
teaching point: this bank's answers state access level with the
claim.

**Q5. "How do the lab frameworks interact with the law?"**
Voluntary commitments vs binding obligations; the honest answer is
that the interaction is still forming, and the
[careers index](../../roadmaps/careers-index.md) carries the dated
snapshot of the posting-level world that results.
