# The project deep-dive bank — presenting your own work

**Navigate:** [← questions](README.md) · [market](../market/README.md)

**How this bank works.** The deep-dive round is 15-25 minutes on one
artifact. Pick the project that matches your target family (see
[roles](../roles/README.md)), then rehearse the four moves below.

## The four moves

**Move 1 — the one-sentence point.** "P09 asks whether aggregate
reliability survives decomposition." No throat-clearing; the first
sentence is the claim the project supports.

**Move 2 — the method, with its discipline.** What was run, on what
data, with what baselines. The corpus habit: paired ledgers, Wilson
intervals, preregistration where it applies. Link it: the
[example report](../../projects/p01-evaluation-harness/example-report.md)
format is the template for every answer.

**Move 3 — the honest tiers.** The strongest differentiator in this
round. "The not-executed tiers are listed with reasons: compute was
out of scope for X, the data license blocked Y." Interviewers extend
offers on this move more often than on the results — it is the
difference between a demo and an audit.

**Move 4 — the follow-up probes.** Rehearse these explicitly:

- "What would you do with 10x compute?" — say what the tier would
  unlock, not that it would be better.
- "What did you expect that didn't happen?" — every project in this
  corpus has a residual section; use it.
- "What's the strongest objection to your own conclusion?" — the
  [honesty ledger](../../courses/honesty-ledger.md) is the rehearsed answer.
