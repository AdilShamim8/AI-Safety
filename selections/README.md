**What this layer is.** The corpus's answer to "where do I start?"
Four curated selections, each a route through the layers with the
order chosen for a goal — every entry naming what it is for, no
"everything matters" lists.

| Selection | For the reader whose goal is |
|---|---|
| [Security](security-selection.md) | the AI-security family (Ch.13-15, P07, the red-team craft) |
| [Safety](safety-selection.md) | the alignment family (the risk model to the research craft) |
| [Interview](interview-selection.md) | an offer (four weeks, part-time, one artifact deep) |
| [Beginner](beginner-selection.md) | from zero (linear, assumes nothing) |

**How to use this layer.** Pick one selection and follow it in
order; the other layers remain the library. Selections overlap by
design — the interview selection's week-2 artifact IS the family
capstone — and each states its one rule, which is always the corpus's
rule: every claim carries its evidence and its boundary.
