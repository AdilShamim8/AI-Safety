# The safety selection — the corpus's alignment canon

**Navigate:** [← selections](README.md) · [safety researcher](../interviews/roles/safety-researcher.md)

**What this selection is.** The curated path through the corpus for
the safety/alignment family — from the risk model to the research
craft, in the order that builds.

## The path, in order

1. **The risk model** —
   [Ch.01 risk models and research epistemics](../chapters/01-risk-models-and-research-epistemics/README.md):
   what we are worried about, taxonomized, before any technique.
2. **The specification problem** —
   [Ch.03](../chapters/03-specification-reward-hacking-and-goal-misgeneralization/README.md)
   with [P02](../projects/p02-reward-misspecification-and-generalization/README.md):
   reward misspecification shown, not asserted.
3. **Oversight** —
   [Ch.05 scalable oversight](../chapters/05-scalable-oversight/README.md)
   with [weak-to-strong](../research/notes/10-weak-to-strong.md) and
   [P05](../projects/p05-weak-to-strong-or-scalable-oversight-analogue/README.md).
4. **Honesty and deception** —
   [Ch.06](../chapters/06-honesty-uncertainty-and-selective-prediction/README.md),
   [Ch.07](../chapters/07-deception-scheming-and-evaluation-awareness/README.md)
   with the [sleeper-agents note](../research/notes/04-sleeper-agents.md)
   and [alignment faking](../research/notes/12-alignment-faking.md):
   the persistence results that make honesty a property to test.
5. **Interpretability** —
   [Ch.09-11](../chapters/09-mechanistic-interpretability-foundations/README.md):
   the [monosemanticity](../research/notes/18-towards-monosemanticity.md)
   line and [faithfulness vs
   plausibility](../research/notes/16-faithfulness-vs-plausibility.md).
6. **Control** — [Ch.08](../chapters/08-ai-control-and-monitoring/README.md)
   with [P08](../projects/p08-control-and-monitoring-protocol/README.md):
   the safety-case pattern the lab frameworks formalize (FSF 3.1's
   safety-case reviews — SRC-REPORT-048).
7. **Craft** — [Ch.24](../chapters/24-research-practice-and-capstone-execution/README.md)
   plus the [deep-dive bank](../interviews/questions/project-deep-dive.md).

## The courses that pair

[Alignment research](../roadmaps/alignment-research.md) is the route;
the DeepMind AGI-safety course supplies the frontier-lab register,
and the Boaz Barak seminar the theoretical one (companion notes for
both in the courses layer).

**The selection's one rule.** Same as the corpus's: every claim
carries its evidence and access level; every study its falsifier.
The [honesty round](../interviews/questions/honesty-round.md) is
this selection's final exam.
