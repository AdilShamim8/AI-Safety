# The interview selection — the corpus's preparation canon

**Navigate:** [← selections](README.md) · [the interviews layer](../interviews/README.md)

**What this selection is.** The curated path for the reader whose
goal is an offer: the minimum viable sequence through the corpus,
chosen for preparation value per hour, not completeness.

## The path, in order (four weeks, part-time)

- **Week 1 — the market and the role.**
  [The demand signal](../interviews/market/demand-signal.md),
  [skills outlook](../interviews/market/skills-outlook.md), then your
  family's page in [roles](../interviews/roles/README.md). Output: a
  one-page role brief you can defend.
- **Week 2 — one project, deeply.** Pick the family capstone (P06 /
  P07+P10 / P01→P10 / P09 / P10) and run it; write the report in the
  [example-report](../projects/p01-evaluation-harness/example-report.md)
  format. Output: one artifact with honest tiers.
- **Week 3 — the banks.** Theory, behavioral, and the family's
  weighted banks from [questions](../interviews/questions/README.md),
  answering aloud before following links. Output: rehearsed moves,
  not notes.
- **Week 4 — the formats.** The family's take-homes from
  [take-homes](../interviews/takehomes/README.md), timed; then the
  [system-design bank](../interviews/questions/system-design.md)
  prompts. Output: two timed submissions with not-covered lists.

## What this selection deliberately skips

Everything else, at first pass: the full chapter sequence, the other
four courses, the research notes beyond your family's spine. The
corpus is a library; this selection is a route through it. Depth in
one artifact beats breadth in ten — the [deep-dive
bank](../interviews/questions/project-deep-dive.md) exists because
interviewers can tell the difference.

**The selection's one rule.** No step produces a claim you cannot
source: the market pages' figures with their dates, the project's
result with its interval, the banks' answers with their links. The
[honesty round](../interviews/questions/honesty-round.md) trains the
habit; this selection schedules it.
