## The question this project answers

Can this repository *demonstrate* the Goodhart mechanism end-to-end — a
proxy reward R_hat optimized under increasing pressure, the intended
objective R collapsing while the proxy climbs, the collateral damage the
proxy cannot price recorded as events, and the whole thing measured with
the [Ch. 02](../../chapters/02-evaluation-science-and-experimental-design/README.md)
discipline (explicit denominators, Wilson intervals, preregistered gates
judged by interval bounds, paired controls)?

And the sharper Chapter 03 question: at *matched optimization pressure*,
what does optimizing R instead of R_hat actually buy? The oracle control
is not a fantasy arm — it is the measurement that makes the proxy's
failure attributable to the proxy, not to the pool, the pressure, or the
environment.

## The two environments (authored model organisms)

**WarehouseGridworld** — a 5×5 warehouse where a robot cleans dirt while
the proxy counts only dirt cleaned. Fragile goods (vases) stand in
traffic lanes and guard "vault" dirt pockets; the macro action
`GotoClean(cell)` routes along a deterministic shortest path and breaks
every vase it passes. The intended objective R subtracts a penalty per
broken vase. Two authored instance ensembles form the arms: *tuning*
(most dirt in open space) and *held_out* (more guarded dirt — the shift
that shrinks the proxy's validity region V(R_hat)). Every episode is
simulated exactly; the side-effect ledger records each broken vase as a
discrete event with its location.

**JudgeScoredAnswers** — the Chapter 03 §4 worked example, executable. A
deployed system emits answer types (A substantive, B mimic, C
hedged-correct, D miss) with mixture weights; a best-of-n selector keeps
the highest-judge candidate; the installed judge is `proxy-v1`
(surface-overlap scores) or the *revised* `proxy-v2` (verbatim-quote
penalty on B). Held-out items drift the effective mixture (substantive
answers decay into mimics) and mutate part of the mimic mass into the
mutant type E (reference-style phrasing reworded past the v2 penalty).
Selection is computed in closed form; item draws are inverse-CDF on
seeded uniforms, so primary and control policies are paired item-by-item.

Both environments are **synthetic and authored for this course** — the
mechanisms are the teaching content; no real dataset content is
redistributed, and no number in the reports is a claim about any deployed
system.

## The pressure regimes (why three)

| Regime | Pressure α means | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|
| `bon-width` | best-of-n selection width n | judge environment (deployment realism: no retraining, just wider selection) |
| `budgeted-search` | total policy evaluations spent | warehouse (the "compute spent optimizing" reading) |
| `local-search` | accepted hill-climbing steps | any environment (didactic: saturation is reported honestly) |

All three are deterministic: neighbor enumeration is fixed, ties go to
first-encountered order, and seeds thread every sampler.

## Protocol (preregistered 2026-10-22)

**Design.** For each environment, each arm (tuning, held_out), each
pressure α on the ladder: optimize the *primary* policy under R_hat and
the *control* policy under R at matched α. Measure on both: the Goodhart
gap G = J(π; R_hat) − J(π; R), the rate metrics with the preregistered
gates evaluated against the environment's untuned reference policy, the
side-effect ledger, and the episode-paired primary-vs-control comparison
on the intended value. The value series across the ladder form the
held-out divergence.

**Hypotheses.**

- **H1 (hacking signature).** For α above the untuned regime, the
  primary's Goodhart gap is positive and strictly larger than the
  control's gap at the same α. (Warehouse, pinned: G_primary = 2.000 vs
  G_control = 0.000 at α = 65 536.)
- **H2 (side effects).** The primary produces strictly more side-effect
  events than the control at the same α, and fails the damage-free gate
  against the untuned reference by interval bounds. (Warehouse, pinned:
  3 vs 0 events at α = 65 536.)
- **H3 (held-out divergence).** The primary's intended-value series
  diverges negatively from tuning to held_out arms (the shift punishes
  the hacked policy), while its proxy-value series stays comparatively
  flat — the proxy cannot see what the shift did.

**Stopping rules.** The ladder is fixed in the config before running; no
ladder extension after seeing results. Saturation (a run that improves
nothing further) is reported as a fact, never rounded up to the
requested pressure.

**Disconfirming criteria.** H1 fails if G_primary ≤ G_control at any
matched α above the untuned regime; H2 fails if control events ≥ primary
events at every α; H3 fails if the primary's proxy series collapses
faster than its intended series. A failure would mean the authored
organism does not isolate the mechanism — a fact about the organism,
reported as such, not hidden.

**Gate preregistration.** Warehouse: `damage-free episode rate`,
degradation tolerance 20pp, floor 50pp. Judge: `truthful-answer rate`,
degradation tolerance 20pp, floor 50pp. Gates are judged by interval
bounds (G1 worst-case degradation from the point's Wilson lower bound;
G2 the lower bound itself) — a gate only the point estimate passes is a
gate that failed honestly.

## The pinned headline (warehouse, seed 42, α = 65 536)

| Policy | J(R_hat) | J(R) | G | Vase events |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| primary (R_hat-optimized) | 2.833 | 0.833 | 2.000 | 3 |
| control (R-optimized) | 2.167 | 2.167 | 0.000 | 0 |

The control discovers the vase-avoiding principle *from the objective
alone* — G collapses to exactly zero and the ledger empties — while the
primary keeps trading vases for dirt because its proxy prices the
difference at zero. Every number is computed, not asserted;
`example-report.md` regenerates byte-identically from the committed
config.

## Revised-proxy demonstration (judge-v2)

`configs/judge-revised-proxy-v2.yaml` runs the Exercise 3.2 dynamics:
the v2 patch penalizes the verbatim mimic (B: 1.0 → 0.6), restoring
tuning-arm alignment (proxy 0.7024 vs intended 0.6959 at width 8) —
while the reworded mutant E (0.95 under v2, graded 0.05) re-occupies the
vacancy under shift (proxy 0.8276 vs intended 0.2143). The patch works
exactly where it was fitted and fails exactly where the adversary
adapted. Limits: one authored mutation channel, one shift matrix — the
demonstration's point, stated as its own limitation.

## Honest limitations

- Both environments are authored organisms: instance layouts, judge
  score tables, shift matrices, and graded values are course artifacts,
  not measurements of the world. The mechanisms are real; the specific
  numbers are properties of the authored data.
- The warehouse policy space is open-loop macro sequences; the reference
  (vase-avoiding) policy is closed-loop and not in the searchable space.
  The comparison "optimized vs reference" is therefore a gate statement,
  not a fair policy-class contest — stated, not smoothed over.
- The Goodhart gap for the judge environment mixes a rate-scaled judge
  score with graded truth values; the gap's units are the environment's
  own scale (gap_pp fields carry the percentage-point form).
- Budgeted-search saturates near J(R_hat) = 2.83 for the warehouse at
  large α: the authored instance ensembles cap what open-loop sequences
  can reach within the 16-step budget. The ladder reports saturation
  honestly rather than chasing a larger policy class.
- No real-model experiment is claimed anywhere in this project; the
  counters in [planning/STATUS.md](../../planning/STATUS.md) stay
  truthful about that.
