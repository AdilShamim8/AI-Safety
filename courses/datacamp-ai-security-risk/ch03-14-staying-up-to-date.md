## Where this lesson sits

The drill for the forward-looking material: the staying-current toolbox and the cost of falling behind — the exercise tests the maintenance posture.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. The toolbox and what each channel is for

Conferences (the field's state of the art, in person); security researchers' work (the earliest warnings); relevant publications (the curated record); the broader AI security community and online forums (the ongoing conversation). The exercise matches needs to channels: 'earliest signal on a new attack' → researchers; 'structured ongoing education' → publications; 'community verification' → forums.

### 2. The price of stagnation

The video's cost list, as the exercise's wrong-answer engine: outdated defenses are more vulnerable to attacks, data breaches, and compliance issues — eroding trust, costing money, hindering innovation. Any stem where an organization stops updating and expects security to hold is the marked failure.
