## Where this lesson sits

The alignment scenario game: steer an organization's security decisions down its own strategic avenue — every round a decision, every decision scored against the strategy it must serve.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Score against the strategy, not the textbook

The game's rule: a measure is right when it fits the organization's goals and appetite, wrong when it imports another organization's posture. The innovation-heavy firm gets agile threat modeling and accepted risk; the trust-heavy firm gets strict protocols and slower innovation — and swapping the playbooks between them is the losing move the game grades hardest.

### 2. The avenue is the strategy

The title's claim: alignment is a road you stay ON, not a destination — the game's final round tests whether, under a strategy shift mid-game, the security posture shifts with it. Rigidity is the last failure mode.
