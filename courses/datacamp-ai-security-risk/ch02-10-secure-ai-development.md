## Where this lesson sits

The drill for the data practices: given a risk or a requirement, choose the practice — the exercise tests purpose-matching across the four.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Match the practice to its purpose

The four data practices sort by what they are FOR: provenance/traceability answers 'where did this data come from and what happened to it' (the anti-poisoning practice); validation answers 'is this data accurate, relevant, untampered' (the pre-ingestion gate); minimization answers 'do we need this much' (the breach-surface reducer); encryption/anonymization answers 'what happens if it leaks anyway' (the last line against exposure and inversion).

### 2. The distractor pattern

Exercise stems that pair a practice with the WRONG purpose are the graded content: minimization does not detect tampering; validation does not remove identifiers; encryption does not tell you the data's origin. If the purpose sentence does not follow from the practice's mechanism, reject the pairing.
