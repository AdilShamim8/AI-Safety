## Where this lesson sits

The chapter's scenario game: implement a security action plan for the fictional organization Landora — prioritizing findings, sequencing mitigation, and communicating the plan: the strategy chapter's capstone in play form.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. The implementor's sequence

The winning procedure: prioritize by severity times likelihood under the budget; take the low-hanging fruit first to show progress and boost morale; weigh cost against expected loss for the expensive items; reconcile stakeholder interests in the plan's wording; and publish the multi-level plan — strategic level down to implementation detail.

### 2. What the game actually grades

Not the plan's cleverness but its alignment: every round scores whether the chosen action serves Landora's stated goals at its stated appetite — the chapter's title claim (strategic INTEGRATION) made playable. A brilliant action misaligned with the strategy loses to a modest action aligned with it.
