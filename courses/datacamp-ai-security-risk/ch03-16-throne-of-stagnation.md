## Where this lesson sits

The course's final scenario game: the kingdom that stops adapting — the cost of stagnation played out against the cost of change, the whole course's argument in one last game.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Stagnation versus adaptation, scored

Each round pairs a change (new regulation, new threat, new capability) with a response (adapt or hold). The game scores the HOLD moves by their compounding costs — vulnerability, breach, compliance, trust — and the ADAPT moves by their trade-off prices. The winning line is the video's: adaptive leadership, proactive strategy, security in motion.

### 2. The throne is the trap

The title's warning: the throne is comfortable — stagnation feels like stability and prices like fragility. The game's final rounds make the accumulated cost visible at once, which is the argument the entire course has been building: constant change is the condition, not the exception.
