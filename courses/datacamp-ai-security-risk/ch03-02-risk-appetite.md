## Where this lesson sits

The drill for the appetite concept: given an organization profile, reason about how much risk it can accept — the exercise tests the determinants, not the label.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. The four determinants

Industry, financial strength, strategic goals, and risk management capabilities — the video's list. The exercise pairs profiles with appetites: the startup accepting more risk because growth is the strategic priority; the bank with stronger finances and capabilities but trustworthiness as the priority. Answers must cite the determinant, not the vibe: 'it accepts more risk BECAUSE its strategy prioritizes growth'.

### 2. Appetite is a position, not a virtue

The graded insight: neither end of the spectrum is 'more correct' — the position follows from the organization's circumstances, and the exercise scores consistency between profile and position. The repo's glossary row on risk appetite is the same claim in expert register.
