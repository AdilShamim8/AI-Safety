## Where this lesson sits

The deep drill on one role: the data science team's specific duties in the security culture — the exercise the course's own emphasis on DS teams earns.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. The DS team's three duties

Monitor model health and integrity so systems perform as expected without bias or error over time; identify model drift and unreliable outputs; identify and correct vulnerabilities in AI algorithms that could be exploited. The exercise discriminates these from neighboring duties: infrastructure vulnerabilities are IT/Security's; legal exposure is Compliance's; the DS line is the MODEL's behavior and its algorithms.

### 2. Why the course singles this role out

Because the DS team owns the model lifecycle, it is the only role that can run the chapter-2 monitoring posture — bias testing, drift watching — inside the chapter-3 culture. The exercise is the join point of the two chapters.
