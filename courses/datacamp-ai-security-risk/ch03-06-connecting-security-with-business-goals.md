# 3.06 — Connecting security with business goals

*Course companion note · [AI Security and Risk Management](README.md) ·
lesson 6 of chapter 3 (interactive exercise, 50 XP) · [course page](https://app.datacamp.com/learn/courses/ai-security-and-risk-management)*

## Where this lesson sits

The drill for the alignment video: map security measures to the business goals they serve — the exercise tests direction of fit.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Measure serves goal, never the reverse

The mapping the exercise grades: encryption serves a trust or compliance goal (and prices agility); agile threat modeling serves a speed-to-market goal (and prices assurance depth); strict protocols serve loyalty and regulatory goals (and prices innovation speed). The wrong answers reverse the direction — choosing a goal to fit a favorite measure.

### 2. Every correct answer prices the second face

The video's two-sides rule carries into the exercise: a mapping that states the benefit without the cost is incomplete. Full credit answers carry both — 'this measure advances that goal at this price'.
