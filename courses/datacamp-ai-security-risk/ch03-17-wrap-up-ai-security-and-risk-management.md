## Core ideas, explained

### 1. The recap, chapter by chapter

Chapter 1: AI has its own unique set of security risks; due to AI's complexity and unexpected outcomes, it is crucial to consider and involve different perspectives. Chapter 2: risk-management fundamentals — AI development and deployment risks, external threats, and methods for discovering and countering vulnerabilities. Chapter 3: the integration of AI risk management with organizational strategy, taught through the start-up-versus-bank example.

### 2. What next, per the instructor

Staying up to date matters in a dynamic field. The recommended follow-ons: AI Ethics, Artificial Intelligence (AI) Strategy, Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) Concepts, and the instructor's other course, Introduction to Data Security. The instructor also invites learners to the Data Girl and Friends initiative — empowering young people through activities supporting awareness and critical thinking in an AI-driven digital world.
