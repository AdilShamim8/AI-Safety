## Core ideas, explained

### 1. Why formal assessments, and what they contain

They establish a baseline understanding of potential risks; involving diverse stakeholders gives a comprehensive perspective; regular assessments let the system adapt as circumstances change; and they support legal and regulatory compliance, minimizing losses, penalties, and reputational damage. The elements: identify possible risks (privacy issues, biases, security vulnerabilities); analyze how likely and how impactful; evaluate against severity and the company's risk tolerance; then develop the strategy to manage, reduce, or eliminate.

### 2. The EU AI Act's risk tiers

In the EU AI Act adopted in March 2024, risk assessment is the cornerstone of regulating AI development and deployment. Systems are grouped by riskiness: unacceptable risk, high risk, limited risk, minimal risk — and mitigation strategy follows the classification. High-risk AI systems require a risk assessment and risk management system; low-risk systems allow alternative measures such as following relevant codes of conduct.

### 3. Internal checks and balances

The other prong: continuous monitoring to catch emerging risks or unexpected model behavior, with checks set from the formal assessment's results. Named examples: regular bias testing to identify unfair outputs, and monitoring for unexpected behavior to catch performance degradation. These ongoing checks act as an early warning system — issues identified and addressed before they escalate — fostering a safety-conscious culture.

### 4. Not ticking boxes

The video's closing argument: formal assessments provide the path for responsible development; internal checks are the guardrails that keep you on it. Effective AI risk management is a culture of continuous vigilance supported by formal controls — compliance with current regulation such as the EU AI Act, adaptability to emerging challenges, and the combination of formal assessment with day-to-day monitoring that builds resilient, trustworthy AI applications.
