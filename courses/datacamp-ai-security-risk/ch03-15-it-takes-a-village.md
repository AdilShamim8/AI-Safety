## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Future topics are ecosystem topics

Regulation, synthetic data, and AGI each implicate the four pillars: people (who tracks them?), legal (what do they require?), technology (what do they change?), processes (what must be updated?). The exercise grades whether the learner routes each future topic through the pillars rather than treating it as a technical curiosity.

### 2. The village, again

The title repeats the culture video's thesis in the future tense: staying current is a collective act — the channels in the previous exercise are people, and the exercise marks answers that assign the future to one team.
