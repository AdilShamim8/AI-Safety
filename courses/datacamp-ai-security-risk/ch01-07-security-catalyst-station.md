## Where this lesson sits

The chapter's closing scenario game: choose the right AI security application for a series of situations — the chapter-one judgment exam.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. The four-step decision the game embeds

For each situation: (1) what is the object — behavior, adversary intelligence, workflows, or events? (2) what is the latency requirement — real-time flags or analyzed hindsight? (3) what must remain explainable to humans, since accountability follows the inspectable path? (4) does the benefit survive the weighing against fairness and privacy costs? A choice that survives all four is a defensible answer.

### 2. The cost of a wrong catalyst

The game's stakes are the operational ones: picking SOAR where SIEM was needed automates a process around undetected events; picking anomaly detection where threat intelligence was needed studies your own users while the adversary walks past. The repo's phrase for the underlying error: solving the problem one instrument down the chain from where it lives.
