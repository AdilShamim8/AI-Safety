## Where this lesson sits

The external-risks capstone game: an attack arrives; triage it under pressure and choose the response — the chapter's blue-team scenario.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Triage = classify, then counter by group

The winning procedure is the drill's two-step with a response bolted on: name the target and phase, then pick the practice that counters THAT group — sanitization for poisoning, input hardening for adversarial, isolation and rotation for theft, absorption and filtering for infrastructure floods, provenance and patching for supply chain.

### 2. The peril is the misread

Every losing move in the game is a misread of the phase — responding to a poisoning incident with input filters, or to a flood with model retraining. The game makes the cost of the misread immediate, which is the point of grading it.
