## Where this lesson sits

The drill for the risk tiers: classify systems into unacceptable / high / limited / minimal risk, and attach the obligations that follow.

## The concepts this exercise tests

### 1. Classify by use, then attach the duty

The exercise gives system descriptions; the task is the tier, then the consequence: unacceptable-risk systems are banned; high-risk systems require a risk assessment and risk management system; limited/minimal risk systems take alternative measures such as codes of conduct. The repo's governance chapter carries the same tier table with the Act's own categories and examples.

### 2. The trap: capability words

Stems describing what a system CAN do are bait — the tier follows the USE and the riskiness of the deployment context, not the capability. An exercise answer that tiers a system on its model size or its cleverness is the distractor; the Act's logic (as the video states) puts risk assessment at the cornerstone for development AND deployment.
