# The lecture library's honesty ledger

*Part of the [lecture library](README.md) · 2026-10-27*

What this library did NOT do, stated as plainly as what it did:

- **No video was watched.** All 57 companion notes are grounded in
  the lectures' announced scopes — titles, course-page schedules,
  published reading lists, experiment descriptions — never in the
  spoken content. If a lecture surprised its audience beyond its
  syllabus, the note cannot know.
- **No transcripts.** YouTube's transcript channels returned
  LOGIN_REQUIRED to this environment's fetches (both web and android
  clients, 2026-10-27) — recorded here as the technical reason, not
  an excuse: the repo's rule predates the attempt.
- **Playlist structure is perishable.** The fetch (2026-10-27)
  captured 19/16/8/14 videos; courses add recordings on delay. The
  Fall 2026 CS 2881 lectures listed as announced-not-yet-published
  were verified as announcements, not deliveries.
- **Reading-list URLs were not individually re-verified.** The
  course pages' own links are the linking source (SRC-CURR-012);
  only the readings this repository already registers carry verified
  URLs here. Others are attributed by title and author.
- **View/subscriber counts were not recorded.** Perishable display
  values, deliberately omitted.
- **The pedagogy analyses analyze architecture, not delivery** —
  what the published structures show about how each course organizes
  knowledge.

- **No DataCamp exercise was opened.** The fifth course (added
  2026-11-03) is interactive and account-walled: the unauthenticated
  course URL redirects to sign-in (recorded as CLM-0212). The
  companion notes rest on the public catalog page (SRC-CURR-014,
  page-inspected) and the owner-supplied synopsis of the lesson list
  and video scopes (SRC-CURR-015, metadata-only). No video was
  streamed; no exercise content is reproduced; the exercise notes
  reconstruct the concepts the exercises test from the neighboring
  videos' announced scopes — stated as that, never as more.
- **The 44 worked examples are original.** Each is this repository's own beginner walk-through of the lesson's announced concepts (2026-11-03); none reproduces exercise content, quotes a video, or paraphrases account-walled material.
- **No CampusX video was watched.** The sixth course (added 2026-11-03)
  is paid and its videos sit behind a paywall; the public course page
  was fetched and read in full (SRC-CURR-016, page-inspected, two
  sessions), the platform marks the outline TENTATIVE itself, one
  lesson is listed with duration 0 — announced, not published, its
  note carries that status — and the companion notes ground in the
  page's published description. Nothing behind the paywall is
  reproduced anywhere.
