# The CS 2881 reading lists × this repository's spine

*Part of the [Harvard CS 2881 companion](README.md) · 2026-10-27*

How much of the course's published reading material does this
repository already carry? The honest map, by register entry:

**Already registered and noted** (read the repo's own note alongside
the course's reading): InstructGPT (note 26), Constitutional AI
(30), DPO (25), GCG universal suffixes (31), AgentDojo (22), CaMeL /
Defeating Prompt Injections by Design (21), Alignment Faking (12),
Scaling Laws for Reward Model Overoptimization (37), Monitoring
Reasoning Models (43-related), TruthfulQA (registered; dataset card
DS-001), Goal Misgeneralization (registered), Discovering Latent
Knowledge (registered), Towards Monosemanticity (18), Scaling
Monosemanticity (3), Sleeper Agents (4), the Anthropic RSP
(announcement page), OpenAI Preparedness (structure page), NIST AI
RMF (landing page), the EU AI Act timeline.

**Covered by dataset cards:** TruthfulQA, BBQ, WildChat, AgentHarm,
InjecAgent, BiPIA, FigStep, AgentDojo (as evaluation environments),
and — added with this library — GDPval, RE-Bench, the METR
time-horizons, and Canaries.

**Not yet carried, honestly listed:** the practitioner essays
(Horace He's compute piece, the transformer-math pair, Lambert's
chapters, Spinning Up), the economics working papers (Turing Trap,
Canaries' PDF itself, the Jones pieces — the cards carry the
findings, not the papers), the LessWrong/AF essays (AI 2027, Normal
Technology, Carlsmith), and the incident-specific reports (UK AISI
model hacking, METR's Hugging Face investigation). Each is attributed
in the lecture notes where the course assigns it; the register's
price-of-admission rule (verify before citing) governs any future
addition.
