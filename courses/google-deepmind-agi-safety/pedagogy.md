# How the AGI Safety Course organizes knowledge — the pedagogy note

*Part of [Google DeepMind — the AGI Safety Course](README.md) ·
written from the verified course structure (2026-10-27)*

## The architecture, stated plainly

Sixteen videos, ~85 minutes, three parts, two exercises. The
organizing unit is **the single named claim**: each video is one
mechanism or one risk, stated at whiteboard speed. There are no
lecturers visible in the format's structure to manage, no seminars,
no assessment beyond the two checkpoint quizzes. The form is a
*compressed statement*, and the compression is the pedagogy:

- **Part 1 (risk model, 5 videos + 2 exercises):** the claims that
  make the problem real — capability trajectory, instrumental
  planning, misaligned-goal genesis — with exercises placed exactly
  where a misconception would compound.
- **Part 2 (research program, 7 videos):** one video per candidate
  answer — the alignment-portfolio structure rendered as a
  curriculum.
- **Part 3 (institutions, 3 videos):** the layer no single model can
  solve.

## What the organization teaches

Three design claims. **A frontier lab believes a general technical
audience can absorb its risk model in 85 minutes** — the compression
is a statement about what is essential, and the selection (what made
the cut, what did not) is the lab's own prioritization made public.
**Exercises are the integrity check of compressed teaching** — with
no seminar, no grade, no discussion, the quizzes are the only place
a false sense of understanding can break. **A lab's curriculum is a
primary source** — teaching the course is itself an institutional
act; this library registers and prices it as such.

## The information-gathering model

The course gathers nothing live: it is a fixed artifact, distilled
from the lab's research program and published as a statement. Its
management problem is **versioning the truth** — the field moves
faster than any fixed course, and the honest viewer treats the
course as a dated snapshot (2026-10-27 in this library's register) of one
lab's view, to be checked against the current literature the way
this repository checks any dated source.

## What this repository adopted

The compressed on-ramp as a legitimate format (START_HERE and the
primer pass in the watch guide), and exercises-with-checkpoints as
the design pattern for any short-form teaching material this repo
ships.
