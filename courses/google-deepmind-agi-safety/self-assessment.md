# The AGI Safety Course — the self-assessment

*Part of the Google DeepMind AGI Safety Course companion · 2026-10-27*

Twelve questions on the course's sixteen parts. They are
answerable from the videos' announced scopes plus this repository's
chapters; several are deliberately the kind the checkpoint exercises
drill, extended one level deeper.

## The instrument

**Part 1 — the risk model (1–5)**

1. State the capability-trajectory claim carefully: what is claimed,
   and what is deliberately NOT claimed about dates?
2. Give the instrumental-convergence argument in three sentences,
   and explain why it is goal-agnostic.
3. Name the four genesis channels for misaligned goals, and match
   each to the mitigation that addresses it.
4. Why is goal misgeneralization the channel that in-distribution
   evaluations cannot catch, by construction?
5. For each failure vignette type (specification error,
   misgeneralization, feedback misspecification, instrumental
   behavior), state which chapter of this repository owns the full
   treatment.

**Part 2 — the mechanisms (6–10)**

6. What supervision problem does amplified oversight address, and
   what structure do debate designs bet on?
7. Why is robustness a safety property rather than a performance
   property? What does deployment do to the distribution?
8. State the control paradigm's core assumption, and what
   trusted/untrusted separation buys in one sentence.
9. What are the two evidentiary channels interpretability opens, and
   why is the internal channel in principle stronger for absence
   claims?
10. What does "safer design patterns" mean that "more training"
    does not — and which repository chapter is its applied depth?

**Part 3 — the institutions (11–12)**

11. Why does the course place institutions INSIDE the alignment
    program rather than beside it? What does the technical stack
    assume that institutions supply?
12. What is the uplift measurement frame for dangerous-capability
    evaluations, and what does an eval establish — and not
    establish — about deployment safety?

**Pointers**: 1–5 — the part-01 notes and Ch. 01/03; 6–10 — the
part-02 notes and Ch. 05/08/09/14; 11–12 — the part-03 notes and
Ch. 20/23.

## How to use this

Work each item in writing before looking at the pointers. The
questions are calibrated so that a learner who has only watched the
videos will find several of them sharp, and one who has also worked
the repository chapters will find them fair. That gap is the
diagnostic: it is what "watched" leaves out, measured.
