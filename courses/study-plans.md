specializations over the common methodological spine. The lecture
library integrates with each:

### The AI-security-engineering route

Front-load CS 2881 Lecture 3 (robustness, jailbreaks, prompt
injection, weight security) with the repo's Ch. 12–15 block; add the
DeepMind P2.4–P2.6 videos (control, interpretability, design
patterns) at Ch. 14; close with Safeshield M7 and M12–M14 for the
compliance wrap of security work. The full mapping lives in each
chapter's watching-companions block.

### The evaluations-and-assurance / audit-only routes

Run Safeshield as a second spine: modules 1–2 with Ch. 01; module 8
with Ch. 01–02; modules 9–11 with Ch. 23; modules 12–14 with
Ch. 22. Pair the CAIS course (Lectures 1, 4, 7, 8) for the
society-scale framing, and the DeepMind P3 videos for the frontier-
evaluation connection. The audit-only route's
[own page](../roadmaps/audit-only.md) carries the week-by-week plan.

## The pacing arithmetic

- **Primer pass:** DeepMind course end-to-end — 85 minutes, once,
  before Ch. 01.
- **Alongside pass:** 3–5 videos per chapter-cluster — roughly 4–6
  hours across the whole course if you take only the primary pairings
  above; 12–15 hours if you watch every listed lecture.
- **Operator pass:** Safeshield end-to-end — 12.5 hours, best spread
  as one module per study session alongside Ch. 22–23.

## The discipline that holds it together

One rule from the library's index: the lectures are **primary
sources, not summaries of this repository**. When a lecturer and a
chapter disagree, the disagreement is the finding — record it,
price both sides, and carry it into the chapter's critique exercise.
The lecture library exists to make that collision productive.
