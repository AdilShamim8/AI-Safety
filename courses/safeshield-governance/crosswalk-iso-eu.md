# Crosswalk — ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act

*Part of the [Safeshield governance companion](README.md) · 2026-10-27*

The compliance question practitioners actually ask: does a 42001
certificate help with the Act? Short answer: it is the best
currently-available scaffolding for the management-system portions of
conformity — and it is not conformity itself.

| Dimension | ISO/IEC 42001 | EU AI Act |
|---|---|---|
| What it governs | The organization's AI management system | Products and practices placed in the EU market |
| Who must engage | Anyone seeking certification (voluntary) | Anyone in scope (mandatory) |
| Evidence style | Process records, audits, continual improvement | Technical documentation, conformity assessment, registration |
| High-risk fit | The AIMS supplies the process skeleton Annex-quality documentation hangs on | Annex requirements define what the documentation must contain |
| Auditor relationship | Accredited certification bodies | Notified bodies (member-state designated) |
| Change speed | Stable (ISO cycles) | Moving target (staged application, evolving guidance) |

**The honest reading.** A 42001 certificate evidences that an AI
management system exists and operates — which most high-risk
obligations presuppose — but the Act's product-safety logic
(conformity per system, per intended purpose) needs artifact-level
documentation the standard only shapes. Practitioners running both
say the sequence matters: build the AIMS first, then let each Act
obligation pull its evidence from it. The repo's
[Ch. 22](../../chapters/22-safety-engineering-and-assurance/README.md)
(the assurance document set) and [Ch. 23](../../chapters/23-governance-and-standards/README.md)
are the conceptual depth for exactly this workflow.
