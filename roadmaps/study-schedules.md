# Study schedules

Three schedules over the same routes. Pick by hours-per-week you can actually
sustain, not by ambition; the adaptation rules at the bottom tell you what to
cut when life interferes. Route sequences live in the
[route pages](alignment-research.md); this page fixes the calendar arithmetic
and the checkpoints.

Assumptions stated up front: chapter hours are medians for a learner who
passed the relevant diagnostic tasks; project hours are for the **smoke or
pilot tier** (full research tiers scale up, and the project README states by
how much); "done" is defined by artifacts, never by weeks elapsed.

## Schedule A — focused 12 weeks (~10 h/week, ~120 h)

A deliberate **selection**: the spine, one route's first four chapters, two
projects, and a mini-capstone. It covers 7–8 of 24 chapters and does not
pretend otherwise. Security-route layout (others substitute per their page):

| Week | Focus | ≈ h |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ch.01 + diagnostic follow-ups | 10 |
| 2 | Ch.02 + preregistered plan | 10 |
| 3–4 | P01 harness, smoke tier | 20 |
| 5 | Ch.12 | 10 |
| 6 | Ch.13 | 10 |
| 7–8 | Ch.14 + P07 build phase | 20 |
| 9 | P07 evaluation phase | 10 |
| 10 | Ch.15 + report with denominators | 10 |
| 11 | benign/attack re-run + error analysis | 10 |
| 12 | mini-capstone: threat model + regression suite | 10 |

**Checkpoint gate (end of week 2):** your evaluation plan names its
denominators and splits; if not, week 3 fixes that before any harness code.

## Schedule B — part-time ~24 weeks (~7 h/week, ~170 h)

One full specialization: spine, 8–9 route chapters, three projects at pilot
tier, comparison notes, and the route's full artifact set short of P10.

| Weeks | Block |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | spine: Ch.01, Ch.02, evaluation plan |
| 4–6 | P01 (full for evals route; smoke for others) |
| 7–16 | route chapters 3–6 with the route's projects at pilot tier |
| 17–20 | remaining route chapters + paper notes (≥8) |
| 21–23 | one comparison note + consolidation of artifacts against route exit criteria |
| 24 | buffer / remediation of weak artifacts |

**Checkpoints:** end of week 6 (one project report passes the
experiment-report template self-review) and week 20 (artifact inventory vs.
route exit criteria — gaps become weeks 21–23 work).

## Schedule C — open-ended research route

All 24 chapters, all 10 projects at full tiers where environment allows,
20+ paper notes, and the P10 capstone or the alternative research capstone.
Realistic total: **350–500 focused hours**. Structure it yourself in
route-order with these fixed points:

1. Spine first, P01 second — every later evaluation runs on that harness.
2. One paper note per chapter from chapter 4 onward (they compound).
3. A narrowing checkpoint before the capstone: a one-page research proposal
   with nearest baseline and falsifiable question.
4. The three self-review passes at the end (curriculum coherence, scientific
   validity, engineering reproducibility — labeled as self-reviews).

## Adaptation rules (what to cut, in order)

1. **Falling a week behind:** cut paper-note polish, not exercises — notes
   can be revisited; skipped exercises hollow out the artifact.
2. **Falling a month behind:** move the last route chapter into "audit-only"
   (read + discussion prompts, skip its artifact) rather than compressing
   project tiers — tier compression is how honest experiments become
   theater.
3. **Compute-constrained:** drop to smoke tier for the model-dependent
   projects (P03/P04/P06) and *say so in the report* — smoke-tier evidence,
   honestly labeled, beats pilot-tier claims you cannot support.
4. **Never cut:** the spine (Ch.01–02), split discipline, denominators, and
   the benign controls. These are the course's non-negotiables.

## Honest workload note

The 24 advanced chapters and 10 projects **do not fit** in a focused 12-week
schedule; schedule A is a selection by design. If you need the full
curriculum, that is schedule C, and its 350–500 h estimate is for a learner
already past the prerequisites — add refresher hours on top if the diagnostic
found gaps.
