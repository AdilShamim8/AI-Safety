# First 90 days — the concrete plans

The [careers index](careers-index.md) maps the roles and the market;
this page makes the first 90 days concrete, week by week, for someone
starting the course today. Assumptions: ~8 hours per week (the
part-time schedule), a laptop, no GPU, no paid APIs. If you have more
time, compress; the ARTIFACTS gate each week, not the clock.

All three plans share weeks 1–4 (the methodological spine) — the
audit-only route in [study-schedules.md](study-schedules.md) is the
slow-motion version of exactly this common prefix.

## The honest failure modes

1. **Reading without artifacts.** Every week's gate is a produced
   thing. If Friday arrives with notes but no artifact, the week did
   not happen — lower the scope, never the gate.
2. **Tutorial drift.** The internet offers infinite adjacent courses;
   this one is complete and self-contained. Finish, then diversify.
3. **Skipping the spine** (weeks 1–4) because you "know evaluation."
   The diagnostic's task B is the test; if it took under an hour with
   clean answers, skip — otherwise the spine is exactly what your
   route's weeks will quietly assume.

## Session-2 additions to the Friday artifacts (2026-10-25)
