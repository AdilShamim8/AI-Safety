# Progress trackers — the step-by-step index

**Navigate:** [← the learner's map](../../ROADMAP.md) · [study schedules](../study-schedules.md)

Four routes, four trackers, one discipline: **progress is an
auditable artifact, not an hour count.** Every checkbox names its
evidence; every milestone ends in self-audit questions; the honest
failure mode (reading without doing) is named where it can catch
you. The compact all-roles ladder lives in the
[careers index](../careers-index.md#progress-tracking--the-step-by-step-ladder);
these are the expanded per-route versions. Fork the repo, tick the
boxes in your fork, and let your commit history be the record.

| Route | Role it serves | The special discipline |
|---|---|---|
| [alignment-research](alignment-research.md) | AI safety researcher | falsifiable extensions and honest intervals on every result |
| [ai-security-engineering](ai-security-engineering.md) | AI security specialist | paired attack/benign ledgers or it did not happen |
| [evaluations-and-assurance](evaluations-and-assurance.md) | AI (safety) engineer | running systems with stated price tags |
| [audit-only](audit-only.md) | the reader/judge (policy, procurement, press) | written challenge of what claims do not establish |

**How the milestones map:** M0 (orientation) and M1 (the
methodological spine) are shared across all four — the spine is the
course's non-negotiable core. M2 is the role fork; M3-M5 are the
role's differentiators; M6 (or M4 for audit-only) is the market
step. Total honest duration at part-time pace: six to nine months
per route, artifact-gated — no calendar promise, only artifact
promises.

**The self-audit rule:** a milestone is done when you can answer its
questions out loud without notes. If you cannot, the reading did
not take, and the honest move is to redo the artifact, not tick the
box.

## The lecture library in the progress ladder

Every route's progress file gains one row type in 2026-10-27: the
watching checkpoint — the primer pass (before Ch. 01), the
alongside pass (per chapter-cluster, recorded in each chapter's
watching-companions block), and, on audit and assurance routes, the
operator pass (Safeshield, one module per session). Mark them in
your copy as you would mark a chapter: watched is not worked —
the self-assessments in each course companion are the check.
